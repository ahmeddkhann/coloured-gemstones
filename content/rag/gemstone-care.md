# Gemstone care basics

## Everyday wear
Remove rings and bracelets before heavy housework, gym sessions, or swimming. Sudden knocks and harsh chemicals can chip soft stones or dull polish.

## Cleaning
Use lukewarm water, a drop of mild soap, and a soft brush. Rinse and pat dry with a lint-free cloth. Avoid ultrasonic cleaners unless a jeweller has confirmed the stone and setting are safe — many coloured stones (emerald, opal, tanzanite, turquoise) prefer gentle hand cleaning only.

## Storage
Store stones separately in soft pouches or a lined box so harder gems (sapphire, ruby) do not scratch softer ones. Keep away from extreme heat and prolonged direct sunlight when possible.

## Emeralds and treated stones
Many emeralds are clarity-enhanced with oil or resin. Avoid solvents, steam, and ultrasonic cleaning that can remove fillers. Ask the store if a specific piece has known treatments.

## Opals and organic gems
Opals and some organic materials are sensitive to dryness and sudden temperature change. Avoid long dry heat and harsh detergents.

## When to see a jeweller
If a claw is bent, a stone rattles, or polish looks scratched, pause wear and have the piece inspected. Do not attempt home re-tipping or glue repairs on valuable stones.
