# Coloured Gemstones — store FAQ

## About the store
Coloured Gemstones is a private atelier for natural coloured gemstones. Guests can browse the online catalogue, add stones to a cart, and place an order without creating a customer account.

## Payment
Orders are cash on delivery (COD) or bank transfer by arrangement. Card payments through Stripe or PayPal are not offered in this version of the store.

## Currency
Prices are shown in Pakistani Rupees (PKR), written as Rs.

## Checkout
Checkout is guest-only. You provide name, email, phone, address, and optional notes. After placing an order you receive an order number such as CG-YYYYMMDD-0001 on the success page.

## Stock
Each stone has a limited stock count. You cannot add more pieces to the cart than are available. Sold-out stones may still appear in the catalogue but cannot be ordered until stock returns.

## WhatsApp
A WhatsApp button on public pages opens a chat with the store. Prefer WhatsApp for personalised sourcing questions, holds, and bank-transfer details.

## Shipping and contact
After an order is placed, the store contacts you by email (and may follow up by phone or WhatsApp) to confirm fulfilment. Status updates may also be emailed when an order moves through pending, confirmed, processing, shipped, delivered, or cancelled.
